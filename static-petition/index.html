    Two official channels, in the order that matters: call so you hear where the grievance stands, then write so there is a dated record of your follow-up. Both are the government's own channels. Neither is ours.

    1. Official step

      Call 1100 and quote the grievance ID

      Tamil Nadu CM Helpline

      Toll free, 7 AM to 10 PM, Monday to Sunday, including government holidays. This is the one channel that can tell you the status of a grievance filed by any method — including one filed by someone else, including one filed years ago. Have the grievance number ready, and the mobile number that was used at the time if you have it.

      Call 1100

      Source: Tamil Nadu CM Helpline FAQ, questions 6, 13, 16 and 20.

    2. Official step

      Send a written follow-up by email

      cmcell@tn.gov.in — the CM Helpline cell

      Email is one of the five channels the Helpline itself publishes, and it is the only one that leaves you a written record with a date on it. Each button opens Gmail in a new tab with the text already written, and CCs the official cell.

    What was reported

    This is not a number. These are people.

    The grievance records carry the deaths as figures. Newspapers carry them as names. Here is what was printed, in the papers' own words, with the link to each report so you can read it yourself.

    The Hindu · 27 May 2026

    Schoolboy killed in elephant attack in Nilgiris

    As reported

    S. Misbah, 14, of Paakana Puthurvayal, a Class VIII student, was killed by a wild elephant in the Bitherkad forest range under the Gudalur division on the evening of 26 May. Locals staged a protest demanding ₹50 lakh compensation for the family.

    Read the report

    The Hindu · 26 April 2026

    Forest staff and a police officer assaulted after a man's death in Gudalur

    As reported

    T. Kalaiselvan, 45, died in an elephant attack at O'Valley in Gudalur. When forest department staff went to recover his body, an angry mob assaulted six of them and a police DSP — the people sent to do this work, beaten while doing it.

    Read the report

    New Indian Express · 12 June 2026

    The Gudalur Peacekeepers

    As reported

    Gudalur sits where Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka meet, on a corridor that runs through 31 villages and more than 2,500 households. Forest staff walk these roads at night, on foot, pushing elephants back. Over 500 movement alerts were generated between January and June this year.

    Read the report

    The Hindu · 2 June 2026

    Collector reviews measures after the child's death

    As reported

    After the fourteen-year-old was killed in Gudalur, the Nilgiris Collector met officials of several departments and ordered that compensation to the family be paid quickly. This site does not publish a status for any grievance. This one is in the newspaper, so it is here with its source.

    Read the report

    Every one of these reports names a place in Gudalur, and every one of them ends the same way — a name, a family, a compensation figure or a review meeting. None of them ends with the conflict being over. The permanent answer has not been given yet. It has to be asked for, and it has to be followed up.
