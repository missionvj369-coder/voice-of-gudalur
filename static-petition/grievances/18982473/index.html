    What this record does not tell you

    • Not the current status. Nobody outside the government can look that up, and we will not guess at it. Call 1100 and quote the ID.
    • Not the filing date. We hold the record as filed and have not filled the gap with a guess.
    • Not the petitioner's identity. Withheld on purpose. The person who filed it is not a public record, and reproducing their address would put them at risk for no civic gain.
    • Not a count of people who agree. Reading a number of deaths is not the same as demonstrating support for any action, and this site keeps those two things apart.

    The official route

    Follow up on #18982473 yourself

    These are the government's own channels, in the order that works. Nothing here is sent by us, on your behalf, or under your name — each one is an action you take.

    1. Step 1

      Call 1100 and say the number

      Toll free, 7 AM to 10 PM, every day including government holidays

      Read out 18982473. The call centre is the only party that can tell you where a grievance you did not file yourself has got to, so start here even if you plan to email as well. Ask them to note the call against the ID, and note the date and time you called yourself.

      Call 1100

      Source: Tamil Nadu CM Helpline FAQ, questions 16 and 20.

    2. Step 2

      Put it in writing

      cmcell@tn.gov.in

      Email is one of the five channels the Helpline publishes, and a written follow-up is the one that leaves a trail with a date on it. The text below asks for a status update on an existing grievance and says so explicitly, so it cannot be mistaken for a fresh complaint. Add your name and contact number at the end before you send it.

      Respected Sir or Madam,

I am following up on grievance ID 18982473 concerning human-wildlife conflict in Gudalur, Nilgiris district.

I would like to know the latest action taken on this grievance and the stage it has reached. Please treat this as a request for a status update on an existing grievance, not as a new complaint.

Thank you.
      Open in Gmail

      Source: Tamil Nadu CM Helpline FAQ, questions 6 and 15.
